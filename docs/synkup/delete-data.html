Delete Your Data from SynkUp

✓ Your Privacy Matters

You have the right to delete your personal data from SynkUp at any time. This page explains what data you can delete and how to do it while keeping your account active.

Types of Data You Can Delete

You can selectively delete different types of data from your SynkUp account without deleting the account itself:

📍

Location Data

  • Location history: All saved locations from past meetings
  • Favorite places: Your saved venue preferences
  • Home/Work addresses: Any saved addresses

How to delete: Settings → Privacy → Location Data → Clear Location History

📅

Meeting History

  • Past meetings: Records of meetings you've created or attended
  • Meeting preferences: Your default meeting settings
  • Recurring meetings: Templates for regular meetings

How to delete: Settings → Data & Privacy → Meeting History → Delete All

👥

Contacts and Connections

  • Imported contacts: Contacts synced from your device
  • SynkUp connections: Users you've connected with
  • Blocked users: Your block list

How to delete: Settings → Contacts → Manage Contacts → Remove All

💬

Messages and Feedback

  • Chat messages: Conversations within meetings
  • Meeting feedback: Reviews and ratings you've provided
  • Support tickets: Your communication with support

How to delete: Individual messages can be deleted in-app; bulk deletion via support request

📊

Usage Analytics

  • App usage patterns: How you use the app
  • Feature preferences: Which features you use most
  • Performance data: App performance metrics

How to delete: Settings → Privacy → Analytics → Opt Out & Delete History

Data Deletion Overview

Data Type Can Delete Individually Retention After Deletion
Profile Information Partial (except email) Immediate
Location History Yes Immediate
Meeting History Yes Up to 30 days (for recovery)
Contacts Yes Immediate
Messages Yes Immediate (except in others' conversations)
Payment Information No (managed by payment processor) Per payment processor policies
Analytics Data Yes Anonymized data may persist

How to Request Data Deletion

1Delete from Within the App

  1. Open the SynkUp app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Data & Privacy
  4. Choose Manage My Data
  5. Select the data types you want to delete
  6. Tap Delete Selected Data
  7. Confirm your choice

Processing time: Immediate for most data types

2Email Request

Send a detailed request to privacy@synkup.app including:

  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Your account email: For identification
  • Specific data to delete: List the data types you want removed
  • Timeframe: If you want data from a specific period deleted

Processing time: Within 72 hours

Data Export Before Deletion

Want to keep a copy?

Before deleting your data, you can export it for your records:

  1. Go to Settings → Data & Privacy → Export My Data
  2. Select the data types to export
  3. Choose export format (JSON or CSV)
  4. Download your data archive

Your data will be available for download for 7 days.

What Data Cannot Be Deleted

Certain data may need to be retained even after a deletion request:

  • Legal requirements: Data we're legally obligated to keep (tax records, legal disputes)
  • Security logs: Limited data to prevent fraud and abuse
  • Anonymized data: Data that's been completely anonymized and cannot be linked to you
  • Others' data: Your messages in other users' conversations (will be anonymized)
  • Transaction records: Basic payment records for accounting purposes

Automatic Data Deletion

SynkUp automatically deletes certain data after specific periods:

  • Location data: Deleted after 90 days (unless saved as favorite)
  • Search history: Deleted after 30 days
  • Temporary files: Deleted after 7 days
  • Session data: Deleted after logout

Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR (European Users)

  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to restrict processing

Under CCPA (California Users)

  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to know what information is collected
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination

After Data Deletion

What to expect:
  • You'll receive email confirmation of deletion
  • Some features may reset to defaults
  • You may need to reconfigure preferences
  • Your account remains active with remaining data
