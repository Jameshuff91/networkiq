Delete Your SynkUp Account

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. Please read this page carefully before proceeding.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you delete your SynkUp account, the following will occur:

  • Immediate effects:
    • Your profile will be removed from the app
    • You will be logged out of all devices
    • Your access to the app will be terminated
  • Data deletion:
    • Your personal information (name, email, profile picture)
    • All meetings you've created
    • Your attendance records for meetings
    • Your preferences and settings
    • Your location history
    • Any feedback or messages you've sent
  • What remains:
    • Anonymized analytics data for app improvement
    • Records required for legal compliance (deleted after retention period)
    • Messages in other users' conversations (anonymized)

Before You Delete Your Account

Consider these alternatives:
  • Take a break: You can simply log out and stop using the app temporarily
  • Delete specific data: Visit our Delete Data page to remove specific information without deleting your account
  • Adjust privacy settings: Modify your privacy settings within the app to limit data collection
  • Contact support: If you're having issues, our support team may be able to help

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your SynkUp account using one of these methods:

1Delete from Within the App

  1. Open the SynkUp app on your device
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap Account Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Read the warning message carefully
  6. Enter your password to confirm
  7. Tap Delete Account Permanently

Processing time: Immediate

2Request Deletion via Email

Send an email to privacy@synkup.app with the following information:

  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Include in your email:
    • The email address associated with your account
    • Your full name as it appears in your account
    • A clear statement that you want to delete your account
    • (Optional) Reason for deletion to help us improve

Processing time: Within 72 hours

Account Deletion for Different Sign-In Methods

Email and Password Accounts

Follow either method above. You'll need to verify your identity with your password.

Google Sign-In Accounts

Your SynkUp account will be deleted, but this won't affect your Google account. You may want to:

  • Revoke SynkUp's access in your Google Account permissions
  • Remove SynkUp from your connected apps

Data Retention and Legal Requirements

Please note that we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Legal compliance: Data required for tax, legal, or regulatory purposes (typically 7 years)
  • Fraud prevention: Limited data to prevent abuse of our services
  • Backup systems: Deleted data may persist in backups for up to 90 days

After Account Deletion

Confirmation

You will receive an email confirmation at your registered email address confirming:

  • Your account has been successfully deleted
  • The date and time of deletion
  • Information about data retention policies

Rejoining SynkUp

If you change your mind, you're welcome to create a new account at any time. However:

  • Your previous data cannot be recovered
  • You'll need to start fresh with a new account
  • Your previous username may not be available
